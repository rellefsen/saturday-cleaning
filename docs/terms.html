Terms

These terms cover text messages from Meadowcrest Ward Reminders, operated by RLE Consulting. The messages are reminders for ward activities a person has signed up for. The first activity is volunteer weekly cleaning of a church building.

Agreeing to texts

A person agrees on the signup form for that activity by choosing Text or Email and text, entering a phone number, and checking the agreement box. Choosing Email does not sign them up for texts. Signing up for one activity does not sign them up for a different activity.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency is about one reminder text for each activity they sign up for. For Saturday cleaning, that text is sent around 9:00am Mountain Time on the Friday before the Saturday. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out. No more reminder texts are sent after that. Reply HELP for help. For help, contact Brother Ellefsen. His contact information is in the LDS Tools app. The texting number does not accept phone calls.

What the texts are not

These texts are not marketing, fundraising, or a newsletter. The message identifies itself as Meadowcrest Ward Reminders.
