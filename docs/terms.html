Terms

These terms cover text messages from Meadowcrest Ward Reminders, operated by RLE Consulting. The messages remind families about volunteer weekly cleaning of a church building.

Agreeing to texts

A person agrees by choosing Text or Email and text on the cleaning signup form and entering a phone number. Choosing Email does not sign them up for texts.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency is about one text per Saturday the family signs up for. The text is sent around 9:00am Mountain Time on the Friday before that Saturday. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out. No more reminder texts are sent after that. Reply HELP for help. For help, contact Don Winegar. His contact information is in the LDS Tools app. The texting number does not accept phone calls.

What the texts are not

These texts are not marketing, fundraising, or a newsletter. The message identifies itself as Meadowcrest Ward Reminders.
