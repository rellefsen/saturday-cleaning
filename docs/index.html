RLE Consulting

RLE Consulting operates Meadowcrest Ward Reminders. The messages are reminders for ward activities a person has signed up for. The first activity is volunteer weekly cleaning of a church building.

What the texts are

A family signs up for a Saturday morning. As many as 4 families can sign up for one Saturday. A text is sent only when that person chooses Text or Email and text on the signup form and enters a phone number. Choosing Email does not send a text.

The text goes out around 9:00am Mountain Time on the Friday before that Saturday. Message frequency is about one text per Saturday a family signs up for. Message and data rates may apply.

An example of the reminder:

Meadowcrest Ward Reminders: Reminder: Patel is signed up for Saturday morning cleaning on Nov 21. Supplies are at the building. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. These texts are not marketing or fundraising.

How people agree to texts

People opt in on the signup form for that activity. They do not text a keyword. They choose Text or Email and text, enter a phone number, and check the agreement box. The text opt-in page is the public record of that agreement.

Help
