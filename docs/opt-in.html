How people opt in to Meadowcrest Ward Reminders

RLE Consulting sends these texts. Meadowcrest Ward Reminders are reminders for ward activities a person has signed up for. The first activity is volunteer weekly cleaning of a church building. People opt in on that activity’s signup form. They do not text a keyword.

There is no opt-in keyword

Customers do not text a word to join. Do not treat this as a “via text” keyword campaign. Email is selected when the form opens. A text is possible only after the person chooses Text or Email and text, enters a phone number, and checks the agreement box.

Number the texts come from

Texts are sent from (855) 529-5728. That number does not accept phone calls. No one answers a call to it. Reply by text only.

Agreement on the signup form

Each activity has its own signup form. The person must check a box, which starts unchecked. The cleaning form uses:

I agree to receive Meadowcrest Ward Reminders texts at this phone number from (855) 529-5728.

Next to that box, the cleaning form says:

Meadowcrest Ward Reminders: one reminder text on the Friday before each Saturday you sign up for. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. For help, contact Brother Ellefsen. His contact information is in the LDS Tools app. Do not call (855) 529-5728.

A later activity form uses the same agreement and names that activity and its reminder day. Checking the box signs the person up only for that activity.

The form links to the terms and the privacy policy.

Message frequency and disclaimers

  • About one reminder text for each activity the person signs up for.
  • For Saturday cleaning, sent around 9:00am Mountain Time on the Friday before that Saturday.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • These texts are not marketing or fundraising.

HELP and STOP

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Meadowcrest Ward Reminders: For help, contact Brother Ellefsen. His contact information is in the LDS Tools app. Message frequency is about one text for each activity you sign up for. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Meadowcrest Ward Reminders: You are unsubscribed from Meadowcrest Ward Reminders. No more messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help.

Confirmation

After the person checks the box and chooses Sign up, the page confirms the signup. The confirmation text is:

Meadowcrest Ward Reminders: You are signed up for Saturday morning cleaning reminders. You will get about one text on the Friday before each Saturday you sign up for. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.

A later activity uses the same confirmation and names that activity instead of Saturday morning cleaning.
