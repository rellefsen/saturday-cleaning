Privacy

RLE Consulting operates Meadowcrest Ward Reminders. The texts are reminders for ward activities a person has signed up for. The first activity is volunteer weekly cleaning of a church building.

Information the signup collects

  • Family name
  • Contact name
  • Email address, phone number, or both, depending on the reminder the person chooses
  • The activity date they choose

A phone number is collected only when the person chooses Text or Email and text.

How that information is used

It is used to keep the roster for that activity and to send the reminder the person asked for. Phone numbers are used only for that text. The information is not sold.

Reply STOP to stop texts. Reply HELP for help.

Questions
